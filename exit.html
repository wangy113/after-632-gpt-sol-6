A note on Zaydi and Twelver traditions

Zaydi and Twelver are different traditions within Shi'a Islam; Twelver Shi'ism is prominent in Iran. The modern Houthi movement grew from Yemen's Zaydi tradition in the 1990s. Zayd ibn Ali's uprising came in 740 CE, after this room's last date. Source: Brookings

Not all Zaydi Muslims are Houthis. Source: UNHCR Refworld

These older events do not explain Yemen's war on their own.
