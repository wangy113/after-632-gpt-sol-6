# Phase 0 gray-box review packet

**Status: prepared; no sessions run.** The [Phase 0 plan](../PLAN.md#phases) calls for showing the gray-box room to 3 to 5 students or colleagues and recording whether they notice each date's lead change and how they read it. This packet guides that formative check. Historical and community review of the scene remains open.

## Prepare

- Show the three doorway captures at the same size, in date order. They use the same viewpoint and neutral light: [632 CE](632-graybox.png), [661 CE](661-graybox.png), [680 CE](680-graybox.png). Open one image at a time for the first look; place the current and previous images together when asking what changed.
- Keep the passages, room labels, lens words, and the coding notes below out of view until the first-pass answers are recorded. Do not use the dissolve capture: it already carries a time caption.
- Use an anonymous participant code on a copy of the response sheet below. Record the participant's words, including uncertainty. Avoid explaining or correcting the scene during the first pass.

Say: “These are three views of the same **imagined** room at different dates. They are not a reconstruction of a documented place. Tell me what you notice and what you think the changes might mean. ‘I can't tell’ is useful too.”

## First pass: images only

1. Show **632 CE**. Ask: “What do you notice? What, if anything, might the arrangement suggest? Which details make you think that? What can you not tell from this picture?”
2. Show **661 CE**, then place it beside 632. Ask: “What changed? What, if anything, might that suggest? Which visual detail supports your reading? How sure are you?”
3. Show **680 CE**, then place it beside 661. Repeat the same change, meaning, evidence, and certainty questions.
4. Show all three. Ask: “What seems to stay the same? Which change is hardest to understand? What else would you need to know?”

If more detail is needed, ask only “Can you point to what gave you that idea?” Do not name a prop or supply a historical explanation first.

## Second pass: reading context

After recording the image-only answers, show the [three draft passages and scene notes](https://wangy113.github.io/after-632-gpt-sol-6/#reading). Open each date's “scene notes and sources” disclosure so its “In this room” and “In the record” labels are visible. Ask: “Does the text change how you understand any of the pictures? Which parts of the room now seem helpful or misleading? Which details do you think are imagined, and which statements are drawn from historical sources? What made that clear or unclear?” Record any revised reading separately from the first pass.

The doorway captures do **not** show the 680 empty hook or taller palm clearly. The 661 letter and coin are too small to require identification. Note unexpected readings, especially any inference that an invented object is a historical relic or that lighting explains a date. After all sessions, summarize the count for each lead visual change, common interpretations and confusions, and one concrete scene or text revision to consider.
